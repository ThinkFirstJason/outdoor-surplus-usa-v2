Catalog

Five categories, quoted to your specification.

  • 01

    Playground systems

    Structures, freestanding equipment and replacement components. Safety documentation is the manufacturer's, supplied per item.

  • 02

    Shade structures

    Hip, sail, cantilever, umbrellas and replacement canopies. Engineering stamps for wind and snow load on request.

  • 03

    Shelters

    Park and picnic shelters and pavilions, with stamped drawings for your site's load requirements.

Who we quote for

Four buyers. Four different jobs.

Install over the summer break, not into the fall term.

District work runs on board approvals, bond or facilities budgets and a short install window. We quote playground systems and shade over play areas line by line, with the manufacturer's safety documentation for each item, so the facilities office can take one packet to the board.

Most quoted Playground structures · Shade over play areas · Benches and tables

Quote for a district

Grant-funded? We quote against the award.

Parks projects are often paid from a grant with its own line items and closeout date. We break the quote into lines you can set against the award, and include origin statements where the grant requires them. Small replacements, like a canopy or a grill, can usually go on a purchase card.

Most quoted Picnic shelters · Park grills · Shade sails · Greenhouses

Quote for a parks project

FAR and DFARS clauses, origin per line item.

For installations, campuses and visitor areas. We respond to solicitations within posted deadlines, quote against the clauses in the package and supply country-of-origin documentation per line. IUID marking is available when the contract calls for it.
